Interview-Level Summary
PL_RE_Writeback is the master orchestration pipeline for
Raiser's Edge writeback processing. It reads enabled entity
configurations, applies a circuit breaker to prevent repeated
failures, obtains a valid Blackbaud access token, and then
processes each entity sequentially through a child dispatch
pipeline. Throughout execution it maintains operational state
within PIPELINE_WATERMARK tables, recording RUNNING,
SUCCESS, and FAILED outcomes together with failure counts,
run identifiers, row counts, and timestamps. The design
implements a resilient enterprise integration pattern with
auditing, fault isolation, controlled retries, and reusable
authentication services.


Of all the pipelines you've shown, this is the orchestration layer and operational-control layer. The real architecture is the combination of:

PL_RE_Writeback
        |
        +---- PL_RE_GetAccessToken
        |
        +---- PL_RE_DispatchRequests
                    |
                    +---- Blackbaud SKY API


Together they form a complete, production-grade MDM-to-Raiser's Edge writeback framework.
