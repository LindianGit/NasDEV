READING THE FLOW END TO END
---------------------------
1. Snowflake builds the request (VW_D365_REQUESTS).
2. ADF sends it to Dynamics.
3. Dynamics creates the record and a GUID.
4. The GUID comes back and is stored in XREF and D365_WRITEBACK_STATE.
5. The scheduled pull copies Dynamics into RAW.DYNAMICS.ODS_*.
6. Reconciliation compares the two sides.
7. MDM can later read the Dynamics data as a live source.
